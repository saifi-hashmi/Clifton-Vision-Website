What We Offer

Complete Eye Care
Under One Roof

Our experienced team of optometrists, dispensing opticians, and clinical specialists provide a full spectrum of vision and eye health services.

NHS Eye Examinations
Funded NHS sight tests for eligible patients, with thorough assessments of your vision and overall eye health by our qualified optometrists.
Private Eye Examinations
Comprehensive private examinations with advanced diagnostic technology, including retinal imaging and detailed assessment of eye health conditions.
Designer Eyewear
A carefully curated collection of frames for all ages and budgets — from everyday essentials to statement designer pieces fitted with precision.
Contact Lens Fitting
Full contact lens fitting service including complex prescriptions, specialist lenses for dry eye, keratoconus, and myopia control.
Children's Eye Care
Specialist children's examinations in a friendly, relaxed environment. Early detection of vision issues helps protect your child's development and learning.
Dry Eye Clinic
Specialist assessment and management of dry eye syndrome using the latest diagnostic tools and clinically proven treatment plans tailored to your needs.
